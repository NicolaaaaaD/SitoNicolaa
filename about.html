The Story

Marketing found me
before I had a name for it.

Growing up in Italy, I became obsessed with a simple question: why do some ads work and most don't? It wasn't pure creativity — it was structure, data, and a deep understanding of what makes people act. That obsession turned into a career.

I spent years inside ad accounts of every scale — from local Italian businesses to global brands like Star Entertainment, Taylor Wimpey, Funko POP, and Florence by Mills. Over 1,000 accounts audited across Google, Meta, TikTok, and Amazon. Each audit taught me something new. And I kept seeing the same pattern: most brands aren't spending too little — they're spending wrong.

Today I work with founders and brands who are serious about growth. We don't do guesswork. We build systems that turn ad spend into predictable revenue — the right audience, the right message, the right margin. Whether you're launching a product or scaling what already works, the discipline is always the same.
