2. What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the site to remember your preferences and collect anonymous data about how pages are used.

3. Which cookies we use

This site uses the following tools, all of which only activate after you accept cookies:

  • Google Tag Manager (GTM-KCSZKMWQ) — a container that manages the loading of third-party scripts based on your consent.
  • Google Analytics 4 — measures anonymous traffic: pages visited, sessions, approximate geographic origin. No personal data is collected without consent.
  • Google Ads conversion tracking — records conversions (form completions, clicks) generated by advertising campaigns to measure their effectiveness.
  • Calendly — used to display the booking widget. Calendly may set its own functional cookies when you open the booking form. Their Privacy Policy applies.

No tracking occurs before consent. We implement Google Consent Mode v2: all ad and analytics storage defaults to denied until you accept.

4. Your choices

When you first visit the site, a banner asks you to accept or reject cookies. Your choice is stored in localStorage and respected on every subsequent visit.

You can change your choice at any time by clicking "Cookie preferences" in the footer of any page.

You can also clear cookies directly in your browser settings or via browser extensions that block tracking.
